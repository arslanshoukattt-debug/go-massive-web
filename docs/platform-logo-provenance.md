# Platform logo sources

Downloaded 29 September 2026. Platform marks identify services; they are not newly created certification badges.

- Amazon, Google Ads, Walmart, TikTok, eBay, Shopify: Simple Icons v11 SVGs, https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/npm/simple-icons@11/icons/ (original brand shapes, monochrome).
- Amazon Ads: https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/AdProductsWebsite/images/logos/OG_image_Squid_Ink.png (Amazon's published image).
- Temu: Wikimedia Commons, File:Temu logo.svg, https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Temu_logo.svg .

Existing programme membership descriptions were preserved. The supplied project has no account-issued Amazon SPN/SAS or Google Partner badge artwork; generic platform marks are used alongside the programme labels rather than recreating those badges.
